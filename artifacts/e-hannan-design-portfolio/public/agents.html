Method

HXO (Human Experience Optimization) is a design methodology that starts from human outcomes, not features, technology or business requirements, and tests every system with one question: does it help or hurt the full spectrum of human experience? AXO (Agent Experience Optimization) designs what an AI agent reads, calls and receives.
