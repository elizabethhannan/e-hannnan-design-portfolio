Transcript

Welcome to our 2026 National Summit on Artificial Intelligence. Welcome to our national audience of higher educators.

In this session, our panel—Jean Velonis, Dr. Kathleen J. Kennedy, and Elizabeth Hannan—explores the critical intersection of human agency and machine-led decision-making. As AI increasingly influences education and career pathways, we must ask: can we truly understand, challenge, or correct them?

Today, we will break down this challenge into five essential steps. I'm joined by an expert panel: Jean Velonis from Salesforce, Dr. Kathleen J. Kennedy from the University of Arizona, and I'm Elizabeth Hannan from EarnLane. Together, we bring three unique perspectives on governing AI.

We begin by identifying where the human gets lost in automated systems and discussing the critical threshold between helpful AI assistance and dangerous dependency on machine-generated outcomes.

Furthermore, we examine where to place necessary human checkpoints when AI recommends credentials and how institutional leaders can establish durable boundaries that remain effective despite rapid technological evolution.

In our closing round, we synthesize these discussions into actionable advice for you, focusing on what steps you can take tomorrow to maintain oversight of these systems.

Here are four core takeaways for your campus. Adopt a tiered framework, prioritize functional transparency, demand accountability from vendors, and prepare your workforce to govern algorithms effectively.

This three-layer framework organizes AI impact across platform credentialing, education system decisions, and classroom learning, with a mandatory audit layer in every section to allow for pausing, reviewing, and pivoting.

To turn these insights into practice, follow these five steps. Map the decision landscape, assign clear reviewers, audit your checkpoints, ask vendors hard questions about their data, and build critical skills among your team.

We encourage you to stay connected with our panel and explore the resources provided to continue your journey in responsible AI governance. Thank you for joining our conversation today.
