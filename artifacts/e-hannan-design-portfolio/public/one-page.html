What I See in a Changing Landscape

The convergence of AI restructuring, ESG regulatory mandates, and economic disruption is not a future event — it is the operating condition of 2026. Organizations that treat AI adoption as a technical deployment problem rather than a human governance challenge will produce systems that are both unsafe and inequitable. I have watched this pattern from the inside of enterprise technology for nearly two decades, and I am building the research foundation to address it directly. My interests extend beyond my formal degrees into public policy and economics — because sustainable AI adoption requires systemic frameworks, not just better models.

HXO Methodology — Human Experience Optimization

I developed and have implemented the HXO (Human Experience Optimization) framework across sixteen years of professional practice. HXO is a design methodology that originates with human outcomes — not with features, technologies, or business requirements — and is grounded in psychology and dimensional spatial intelligence. In an AI adoption context, HXO provides the evaluative lens I apply to every system I research and build: does this help or hurt the full spectrum of human experience? It is not a framework I inherited. It is one I built, tested, and refined across enterprise, startup, and academic contexts.
