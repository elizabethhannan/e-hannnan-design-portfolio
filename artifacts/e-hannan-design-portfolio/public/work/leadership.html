When the team includes agents

Agents are managed like team members

  • A written role for every agent: what it may do alone, and when it must hand the decision to a person.
  • Reversible work first: agents act alone only where the result can be undone; a person decides where it matters.
  • Hand-backs with context: task, intent, confidence, what was ruled out and what happens if no one decides.
  • One switch: any agent can be paused, and all agents can be stopped at once.
  • Audit: every action is logged, and the auditor is audited too.
