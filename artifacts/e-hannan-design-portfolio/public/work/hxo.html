Two users, one method

People and agents are both users now

HXO · for people

Starts from the human outcome and tests every system against it. Founded by Elizabeth Hannan and tested over 15+ years with hundreds of clients.

AXO · for agents

Agent Experience Optimization designs what an AI agent reads, calls and receives, so agents act within the same rules people do.

How a team adopts it

Seven steps, starting with one workflow

  1. Listen. Study the people and the agents.
  2. Define. Name the one gap in trust that matters most.
  3. Design both sides. HXO for the person, AXO for the agent.
  4. Prototype. Preview, approve, stop, undo and hand back.
  5. Test. Measure trust with real people, not only accuracy.
  6. Resolve. Agents act where work is reversible; a person decides where it matters.
  7. Audit. Every action is logged, and the auditor is audited too.
