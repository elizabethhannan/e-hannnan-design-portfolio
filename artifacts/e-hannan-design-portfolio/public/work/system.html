Adopt it at your company

Start with one workflow

  1. Listen. People and agents are both users now; study both.
  2. Define. Name the one gap in trust that matters most.
  3. Design both sides. HXO for the person, AXO for the agent.
  4. Prototype. Preview, approve, stop, undo and hand back, from the kit.
  5. Test. Measure trust with real people, not only accuracy.
  6. Resolve. Agents act where work is reversible; a person decides where it matters.
  7. Audit. Every action is logged, and the auditor is audited too.

HXO (Human Experience Optimization) is a design methodology that starts from human outcomes, not features, technology or business requirements, and tests every system with one question: does it help or hurt the full spectrum of human experience?
