Design section 04 · A day away · fisheye gallery and scroll clock

Florilegium

Open full screen ↗

A single day away, told in ten plates from 05:40 to 21:15: a flower at first light, the boardwalk, the trail, the beach, the vines, the bath, dinner at sunset and the piano bar. Design section 05 · The Arboretum · hand-drawn canvas meadow and scroll clock

Phototropism

Open full screen ↗

An ink meadow on warm paper. Every stem, and every letter of the headline, leans toward the pointer as if it were the sun. Click to plant a seed. Scroll carries the meadow from morning through dusk into night, when the flowers close and fireflies come out.

Move the pointer to be the light. Click the ground to plant a seed. Scroll inside the frame to let the sun set.

Design section 06 · The Arboretum · word-particle canvas, blurred colour fields and scroll story

Guestbook

Open full screen ↗

Hundreds of short memories from guests, each one a word on a canvas, grow into a single flower. Scroll opens the bloom, lifts the words off like pollen, then gathers them into the word “return”. Guests can leave a memory of their own; it is planted in a petal, in lime.

Move through the words to push them aside. Type a memory and press Plant. Scroll inside the frame to bloom, scatter and gather the words.

Design section 10 · The Arboretum · AI prompt hero, in-browser forecast and data drop

Horizon

Open full screen ↗

An AI trip forecaster over a misty valley at sunrise. Ask it to predict the quietest week or the holiday room rates, or drop in a CSV of bookings. It fits a trend and seasonality in the browser and draws the history in white and the forecast in amber, with a widening 90% band.

Type a question or choose “or use sample”, then Predict. Hover the chart to read values. Drop any CSV with a number column onto the frame.

Design section 11 · The Arboretum · voice-first agent UI, WebGL orb and live speech

Concierge

Open full screen ↗

A voice concierge for the stay. Tap the mic or hold Space: with permission the orb swells with your real voice and transcribes you; without it, a sample request is heard. The orb twists while it thinks and pulses on every word of its reply. Three voices recolour the orb and the page.

Tap the mic, or type into “or type…” and press Enter. Turn the sound on to hear replies. Switch voices at the bottom.

Design section 12 · Canopy · ESG financial reporting · glassmorphism, live KPI rail and product marquee

Canopy: ESG Financial Reporting

Open full screen ↗

Three product-marketing sections for an ESG reporting platform built for finance teams: a home hero with a scrolling KPI rail, a product hero with the reporting ledger, and an integrations band. Emissions are tied to general-ledger accounts, carbon is priced into the P&L, and CSRD and ISSB disclosures come out audit-ready. Figures are sample data.

Scroll inside the frame through the home hero, product hero and integrations. Hover a button to see the arrow sweep.
