Travel info

Before you fly

Which seats are free?
Every seat is a window or an aisle, and standard seats are free at check-in. Glide members and up choose at booking.
How much can I bring on board?
One carry-on and one personal item on every fare. Your first checked bag is free from Soar.
Can I change my flight?
Changes are free up to 24 hours before departure. Soar and Summit members can make same-day changes.
Do you fly to Halifax nonstop?
Yes. Phoenix to Halifax runs four times a week, and Seattle to Halifax three times a week.
See you at the gate.

Elevated economy, unhurried hospitality. A window or an aisle on every flight.
